SMART BOOKING MOVE & CAPACITY PLANNER

New functionality:
- Move Booking button on Service Manager and Future Booking job tiles.
- One-click move forward by one day or one week, or select any date.
- Retains customer, vehicle, job notes, labour, MOT, parts, VHC and invoice links.
- Keeps the existing technician or allows reassignment while moving.
- Checks total workshop capacity and selected technician availability before confirmation.
- Warns about unavailable technicians and overbooked days while allowing a manager override.
- Records every booking move permanently in the job timeline and bookingMoves audit history.
- Future Bookings and the live Job Board refresh immediately after a move.
- Responsive for desktop and tablet.
