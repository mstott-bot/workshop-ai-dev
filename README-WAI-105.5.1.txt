WAI-105.5.1 — VISIBLE MOVE BOOKING BUTTON

- Adds a prominent blue Move Booking button at the top-right of every live Service Manager, Daily Planner and Future Booking job tile.
- Shows the booking date directly beneath the job heading.
- On tablets the Move Booking button becomes full width so it cannot be hidden among other actions.
- The button opens the existing working move dialogue with new date, technician, reason, capacity checks and audit history.
- Completed/closed jobs remain locked and do not show the move control.
