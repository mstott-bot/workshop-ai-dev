Workshop AI WAI-115.8 — Workshop Brief Expected Revenue

Built from WAI-115.7.

Workshop Brief / Workshop Summary now shows BOTH:

1. Revenue Today
   - Actual ex-VAT invoices issued today
   - Credit notes raised today deducted
   - Remains the true actual revenue KPI introduced in WAI-115.7

2. Expected Revenue Today
   - Based on today's booked workshop jobs
   - Labour = allowed/booked hours × applicable job labour rate
   - MOT = configured MOT selling price added for each booked MOT
   - Mirrors the previous Command Centre expected-revenue logic

Example:
Today's bookings:
- 3.0 retail labour hours at £70 = £210
- 2 MOTs at £54.85 = £109.70
Expected Revenue Today = £319.70

Actual Revenue Today remains separate and only rises when invoices are issued.

No invoice calculations, VHC, CRM, timers, productivity, efficiency, parts or other workflow logic changed.
