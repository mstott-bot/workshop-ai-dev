Workshop AI WAI-115.11 — Productivity vs Utilisation Separation

Built from WAI-115.10.

The Workshop Performance Dashboard no longer gives Productivity and Utilisation the same calculation.

Running-month definitions:

PRODUCTIVITY MTD
Productive job-clocked hours MTD ÷ available attendance hours MTD × 100

UTILISATION MTD
Booked / allocated labour hours for dates from the start of the current month through today
÷ available attendance hours MTD × 100

LABOUR RECOVERY MTD
Invoiced labour hours sold MTD ÷ productive job-clocked hours MTD × 100

EFFICIENCY
Remains sold / allowed labour hours ÷ actual productive job-clocked hours where used.

Example:
Available attendance MTD = 100 hrs
Booked/allocated labour MTD = 92 hrs
Actual productive job-clocked time MTD = 78 hrs
Invoiced labour sold MTD = 84 hrs

Utilisation = 92%
Productivity = 78%
Labour Recovery = 107.7%

This makes the KPIs measure different management questions:
- Utilisation: How full is workshop capacity?
- Productivity: How much available time is actually spent productively on jobs?
- Labour Recovery: How many invoiced labour hours are recovered from the productive time used?

No invoice, VHC, CRM, technician timer, MOT, parts or other workflow logic changed.
