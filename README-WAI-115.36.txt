WAI-115.36 — Technician Handover Notes

- When a technician stops a job timer and confirms more work is still required, Workshop AI prompts for handover notes.
- Notes are stored against the job with technician, date and time and added to the permanent job timeline.
- The latest handover is shown prominently on the Tech View job card and inside the opened job before work continues.
- Handover history remains in the timeline if a job changes technician more than once.
- Existing timer, ready-for-collection, carry-over, messaging and reporting logic is preserved.
