Garage Gurus Workshop — WAI-111.0 Stock Control

Added three new top-level stock areas:
- Parts Stock
- Tyre Stock
- Oil Stock

Parts Stock
- Add description, exact part number, category, supplier, quantity, cost, sell price, minimum stock and location.
- Search by description (e.g. air filter), part number, supplier, category or location.

Tyre Stock
- Add tyre size, brand, pattern, load/speed rating, quantity, cost, sell price, minimum stock and location.
- Search by tyre size, brand or pattern.

Oil Stock
- Add grade, brand, specification, litres in stock, cost per litre, sell price per litre, minimum stock and location.
- Search by grade, brand or specification.

Invoice Integration
- Any stocked part, tyre or oil can be added directly to an open Finance Centre estimate.
- The stock item is carried into the invoice with quantity, cost and sell price.
- Stock is deducted when the invoice is issued (not while it is only an estimate).
- Reopening/crediting restores committed stock so an amended estimate can be re-issued correctly.
- Invoice issue is blocked if there is not enough stock available.

Other
- Live stock count, low-stock count and cost-value cards.
- Manual stock adjustment.
- Low stock highlighting.
- Existing Workshop logic retained.
