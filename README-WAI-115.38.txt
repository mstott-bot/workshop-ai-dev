Lost & Gained Labour Report

Reports Interface > Financial > Lost & Gained Labour

- Shows actual productive hours versus charged/sold labour hours.
- Shows hours lost and labour income lost.
- Shows hours gained and labour income gained.
- Shows net hours and net labour-value position.
- Supports Today, This Week, This Month, Previous Month, YTD and Custom via the existing report period selector.
- Includes arbitrary Month A vs Month B comparison.
- Job detail includes date, registration/customer, job type, technician, actual hours, charged hours, applicable rate and £ impact.
- Uses invoice labour quantity when available; otherwise uses the job's sold labour hours.
