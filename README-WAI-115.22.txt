WAI-115.22 — Customer Waiting Priority

Changes:
- Load Job priority options are now Customer Waiting / Today / Urgent.
- Today is the normal default when the form resets.
- Existing legacy Routine jobs display as Today so old saved data remains compatible.
- Customer Waiting jobs are highlighted prominently in Tech View.
- Customer Waiting jobs sort ahead of other unfinished technician jobs.
- Opening a Customer Waiting job keeps a clear warning visible in the active job screen.
- No timer, invoicing, VHC, CRM, reporting, stock, or other workflow logic changed.
