Workshop AI WAI-102.6.1 – Complete Next Conversation Maintenance List

Changes:
- Next Conversation now shows every applicable maintenance item for every linked vehicle.
- Removed the previous five-item display limit.
- Shows MOT, Service, Major Service, Brake Fluid Change, Gearbox Oil Change, Air Conditioning Gas and Cambelt where applicable.

- Items marked Not Applicable are excluded from Next Conversation.
