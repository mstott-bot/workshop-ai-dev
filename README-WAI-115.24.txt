Workshop AI WAI-115.24 — Workshop Diary

Changes:
- Added dedicated Workshop Diary top navigation tab.
- Moved job booking / Load Job workflow out of Service Team into Workshop Diary.
- Removed Future Bookings section from Service Team; diary is now the single future-booking view.
- Diary 7-day capacity strip calculates technician hours available, labour hours booked, hours remaining, technicians working, and capacity percentage.
- Capacity uses the existing date-specific Tech Team availability / allocated-hours records.
- Diary bookings can be moved, opened, or removed.
- Same-day bookings continue into the existing live workshop/Tech View workflow.
- CRM Create Workshop Job / Load New Job actions renamed to Book Workshop and now route to Workshop Diary with customer/vehicle pre-filled.
- Priority options are Customer Waiting / Today / Urgent; Today is the default.
- Customer Waiting jobs are prominently highlighted in Tech View and sorted ahead of normal work.
- Tyre ordering removed from Service Team panels/action queue; tyre workflow remains in Parts & Tyres.
- Existing workshop timers, jobs, CRM data, invoicing, reports, VHC, MOT, parts and performance logic retained.
