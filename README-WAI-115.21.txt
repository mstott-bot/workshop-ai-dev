Workshop AI WAI-115.21 — Technician-Led Additional Work Authorisation

Built from WAI-115.20.

LOAD NEW JOB / SERVICE TEAM
- Removed the Retail Authorisation field from the new-job form.
- Service Team no longer has to mark a retail job Awaiting / Approved / Declined when the original job is created.
- New jobs start with no additional-work authorisation request.

TECHNICIAN WORKFLOW
- Technician completes the work already booked/authorised.
- If extra work is discovered, the technician does NOT continue automatically.
- Technician selects Waiting for Authorisation from Tech View.
- The job then moves into the existing Awaiting Authorisation workflow.
- Service Team receives the existing prominent notification/action.
- Service Team contacts the customer and then records Work Authorised / Declined / No Answer / Call Back.
- Authorised work notification is sent back to the technician before they continue.

No timer, VHC, invoice, stock, CRM, revenue, productivity, utilisation or other Workshop AI logic changed.
