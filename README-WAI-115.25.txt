WAI-115.25 — Workshop Diary Labour Mix

- Preserves the complete WAI-115.24 Workshop Diary.
- Adds prominent Retail, Warranty and Internal percentage cards for the selected diary date.
- Percentages show each work type's share of the day's booked labour hours, so the visible split totals 100% when work is booked.
- Adds configurable daily diary allocations in Targets (defaults: Retail 60%, Warranty 30%, Internal 10%).
- Target percentages must total 100%.
- Highlights work types nearing or exceeding their daily allocation.
