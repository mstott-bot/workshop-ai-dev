Workshop AI WAI-102.0 — Daily Operational Gross Profit & Historical Reporting

Built on WAI-101.9.

Changes:
- Live Revenue Counter Operational Gross Profit is now the accumulated GP from invoices issued today, less credit notes raised today.
- The value remains for the full day even after vehicles leave live workflow queues.
- It resets automatically when the calendar date changes because each day is calculated independently.
- New Finance Centre report: Operational GP Report.
- Daily, weekly and monthly views.
- Monthly comparison for the latest 12 months.
- Shows invoices, credits, sales ex VAT, direct costs, operational GP and GP percentage.
- Uses invoice issue audit date; credits are deducted on credit-note audit date.
- Direct costs include invoice line costs for labour, parts, oil, MOT, consumables, sublet and other categories.
