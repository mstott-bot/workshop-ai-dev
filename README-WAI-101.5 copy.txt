Workshop AI — WAI-101.5 Technician Cost per Job Report

Added under Reports > Financial:
- Technician Cost per Job report.
- Counts completed jobs only.
- Excludes all MOT jobs from both job count and labour cost.
- Uses each technician's individual hourly employment cost from Finance Settings.
- Calculates actual clocked hours × hourly cost.
- Shows total cost to business and average cost per completed job.
- Includes period and technician filters already used by Workshop Intelligence.
- Includes completed-job drill-down by registration, job number, job type, hours and cost.

Calculation:
Total technician labour cost ÷ completed non-MOT jobs = average cost per job.
