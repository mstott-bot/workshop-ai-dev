WAI-101.7 — Parts Unit & 5W30 Oil Cost Settings

Changes:
- Removed the word "each" from Parts invoice lines, including migrated existing Parts lines.
- Added Finance Settings field: 5W30 oil cost £/litre.
- Quick Add Oil now creates a 5W30 Oil line with unit "litre" and automatically applies the configured cost per litre.
- 5W30 oil cost is protected as an automatic setting value.
- Other oil grades remain fully editable: change the oil description away from 5W30 and enter the appropriate cost manually.
- Quantity × oil cost and quantity × oil sell price continue to feed Cost, Net, GP, VAT and invoice totals.
