WAI-115.31 – Technician Customer Visit Count

- Tech View job cards now show the CRM customer visit count.
- A first visit is clearly marked as “Customer Visits: 1 · First Visit”.
- The active technician job screen also shows Customer Visits.
- Visit count is read from the existing CRM record by vehicle registration, so it updates from the CRM data already maintained by Workshop AI.
- No lifetime spend or other customer financial information is exposed in Tech View.
- Existing workshop, diary, messaging and efficiency logic remains unchanged.
