Workshop AI — WAI-104.0 Customer Portal Foundation

Added a read-only Customer Portal and Digital Vehicle Record.

Includes:
- Portal Publisher inside Workshop AI
- Vehicle search and selection
- Customer access-code generator for the local prototype
- Customer preview
- Vehicle details and latest mileage
- Current workshop visit and progress track
- Completed vehicle history timeline
- Technician findings, repair summaries and write-ups
- VHC event visibility
- Advisories and future maintenance
- Parts and warranty record
- Photos/video summary
- Documents and invoice record placeholders
- Per-vehicle visibility controls

Explicitly excluded:
- Customer authorisation actions
- Online payments
- Booking functions

Important:
This is the working front-end foundation using the existing browser data. A live production portal will require secure server-side authentication, a shared database and protected document storage.
