Workshop AI WAI-102.6.2 — Prospect Centre Upgrade

- Prospect Centre button opens and refreshes a working dedicated CRM screen.
- 30, 60 and 90 day upcoming-work views.
- Contacted checkbox on every prospect row with timestamp storage.
- Text and email actions automatically mark the prospect as contacted.
- Booked In action records booking date and estimated value.
- Declined action records the outcome.
- New Booking Report tab shows customers contacted, bookings made, conversion rate and estimated booking value.
- Booking report lists customer, registration, work booked, contact method, telephone and booking value.
