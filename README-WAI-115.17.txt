Workshop AI WAI-115.17 — Actual Revenue Today

Workshop Summary > Revenue Today now uses actual Finance Centre turnover:

Revenue Today = invoices issued today EX VAT - credit notes raised today EX VAT.

Includes every issued invoice line: labour, MOT, parts, tyres, oil, consumables,
sublet and other items. Payment timing does not affect this figure.

Finance Centre "Paid Today" remains cash received and is intentionally separate.

Workshop Summary also refreshes immediately after a Finance Centre change.

No invoice maths, CRM, VHC, timers, productivity or utilisation logic changed.
