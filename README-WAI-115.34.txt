WAI-115.34 — CRM Texting & Prospect Follow-up

- Adds Text Customer to CRM customer profiles.
- CRM text opens the device SMS composer using the customer's saved mobile number and records the message in Communications.
- Prospect Centre Text action keeps the customer in the active prospect pipeline so a follow-up call/conversation can still happen.
- Texting records the contact attempt and timestamp in the prospect timeline.
- Adds a dedicated Declined view in Prospect Centre.
- Declined prospects can be reopened or removed from the Declined list.
- Removing a declined prospect does not delete the CRM customer or vehicle and prevents that maintenance prospect from immediately reappearing in the active pipeline.
- Existing diary, workshop, CRM, reporting and technician logic retained.
