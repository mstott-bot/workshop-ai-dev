Workshop AI WAI-102.6 — CRM Maintenance Schedule & Prospect Centre

Changes:
- CRM maintenance list reduced to MOT, Service, Major Service, Brake Fluid Change, Gearbox Oil Change, Air Conditioning Gas and Cambelt.
- Existing matching maintenance data is migrated into the new list.
- Cambelt can be marked not applicable.
- Full maintenance schedule is visible on each customer overview for every linked vehicle.
- New CRM-level Prospect Centre tab with 30, 60 and 90 day views.
- Prospect list includes customer ID, telephone number, email, registration, work due, due date and contact status.
- One-click Text and Email buttons open the device messaging/email application with a pre-filled reminder.
- Reminder actions are logged in the customer communications record.

Important:
- Text and email buttons use the device's installed SMS/email application. A direct-send integration requires a connected SMS/email provider in a future production release.
